# Skippi Ice Pops — 3D Garden Selling Simulation

> **A high-fidelity 3D gamified educational simulation built for SkilliZee**  
> Based on the Shark Tank India Case Study: *"Can You Turn Childhood Nostalgia into a Billion-Rupee Business?"*  
> **Grades:** 4–9 | **Format:** Interactive 3D Selling Simulation & Shark Tank Pitch Defense

---

## 🌟 Overview & Concept

Instead of a passive multiple-choice questionnaire, this application places the learner in the shoes of a **Skippi Ice Pops Entrepreneur** in a vibrant, lively 3D garden park. 

The student manages a dedicated Skippi vending stall, interacts with a queue of animated children and parents with distinct personalities, answers real customer questions connecting directly to the POA curriculum, selects the right products, executes animated product handovers, launches summer marketing campaigns, and analyzes sales and financial lessons.

---

## 🎮 Key Features & Gameplay Loop

1. **Lush Living 3D Garden World**:
   - Winding cobblestone promenade, central tiered stone fountain with animated water spray and ripple effects, stylized wind-swayed trees, blooming flowerbeds, park benches, garden streetlamps, entrance arches, fluttering animated butterflies, and floating sunbeam pollen particles.
2. **Dedicated Skippi Vending Stall & Freezer Bike**:
   - Striped canopy roof (yellow, red, cyan), illuminated overhead brand marquee, authentic ice pop showcase with 3D translucent pops (Raspberry, Mango, Orange, Bubblegum, Lemon, Cola), chilled vapor particles, and the famous **Skippi Freezer Bike**!
3. **Animated 3D Salesperson (Ravi)**:
   - Dynamic procedural gestures: welcoming waves, conversation hand gestures, reaching into the freezer box, physical handover animations over the counter, and celebration poses.
4. **Diverse 3D Customers**:
   - Multiple characters with unique heights, outfits, and pedagogical scenarios:
     - **Aarav (Grade 4–5)**: Childhood nostalgia craving and flavor selection.
     - **Mrs. Priya & Daughter Anvi (Grade 6–7)**: Parent hygiene concern (100% RO Purified water, natural colors vs unhygienic street ice).
     - **Kabir (Grade 6–8)**: Party logistics problem (Ready-to-Freeze innovation vs melted popsicles on a 45-min bus ride).
     - **Simran (Grade 8–9)**: Price vs Brand value comparison (Defending ₹15 quality vs cheap roadside ice golas).
     - **Aman / Shark Investor (Grade 9)**: Scalability and off-season demand management (The All-Sharks ₹1 Cr deal).
5. **Skippi Strategic Business Hub**:
   - **Shark Tank Deal Room**: Details of founders Ravi & Anuja Kabra, original ₹45L ask vs historic ₹1 Cr for 15% All-Sharks deal.
   - **Revenue Journey**: Visual chart of reported FY22–FY25 revenue trajectory (₹2.5 Cr → ₹15.4 Cr → ₹20 Cr → ₹8.2 Cr) and critical business lessons on winter seasonality and demand forecasting.
   - **K-Matrix Arena**: Side-by-side comparison matrix of Skippi vs Local Street Golas vs Branded Ice Creams.
   - **Campaign Studio**: Student campaign builder allowing learners to pick hooks and platforms to earn bonus XP.
6. **Interactive Controls & Presentation**:
   - 360° Orbit Camera (Left Click + Drag).
   - Smooth Zoom (Mouse wheel / Pinch).
   - Camera Mode toggle (Stall Focus Close-up ↔ Garden Panorama ↔ Fountain View).
   - Offline Web Audio synthesizer (cash register cha-ching, counter bells, celebration chimes, pops).
   - Fullscreen button and responsive mobile touch controls.
7. **Shark Tank Final Pitch Evaluation**:
   - Comprehensive rubric scoring (30/30 Marks) across 6 categories matching Section 13 of the POA.

## 🚀 How to Run

### Development Mode:
```bash
npm run dev
```
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.

### Production Preview:
```bash
npm run preview
```

### Windows One-Click Launcher:
Double-click `start_simulation.bat` in this folder to launch the server and open your browser automatically.
